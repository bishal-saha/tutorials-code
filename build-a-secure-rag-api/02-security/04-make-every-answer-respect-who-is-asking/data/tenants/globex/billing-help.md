# Invoices and billing

Invoices are issued on the first working day of each month and are available under Account > Billing.

## Changing the billing contact

An account owner can change the billing email under Account > Billing > Contacts. The change applies from the next invoice.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails we retry it three times over seven days and email the billing contact each time. Update the card under Account > Billing > Payment method to stop the retries.
